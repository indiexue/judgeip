About JudgeIP

JudgeIP is an IP intelligence and network diagnostics toolkit. It answers one concrete question: what does the IP you are using right now look like to platform risk engines — residential or hosting, natively registered or re-announced, present on public blocklists, flagged as a proxy or VPN by any source.

Why this site exists

Anyone running cross-border business, working remotely, or simply trying to use an AI service has hit the same wall: the network is fine, yet the platform says no. The reason is rarely connectivity — it is the exit IP itself: its registration, reputation history and flags. This information is public but scattered across databases, and there is no convenient way to check it all at once. JudgeIP turns it into a single lookup, with the source and record behind every conclusion attached.

How it works

  • Open and test instantly. No sign-up, no accounts, no names, emails or phone numbers collected.
  • Every lookup aggregates a dozen public data sources in real time: RIR registration records, BGP routing and RPKI state, four DNSBL blocklists, the IPsum threat feed, Shodan InternetDB and several IP intelligence services.
  • The scoring rules are fully public — every deduction can be verified on the scoring rules page, and the data sources plus caching behavior are documented on the data sources page.
  • The whole site runs on Cloudflare edge nodes. Lookups execute close to the visitor, and results are cached briefly per IP to reduce repeated upstream traffic.

The score is not a verdict

What this site provides is a reference score: it describes the objective traits of an IP in public data, not the actual admission decision of any platform. Risk engines are private, change without notice, and consider signals this site cannot see, such as account behavior and device fingerprints. A high-scoring IP guarantees nothing; a low-scoring one is not proof of wrongdoing — it simply shows what the address looks like in public records. Samples and rankings likewise reflect only this site's own lookup history, not a portrait of the internet.

How this site makes money

It does not, currently. No ads, no paywall; every core feature is free and requires no sign-up. If any form of monetization is introduced in the future (advertising, paid services or partnerships), it will be stated here and in the terms of service — and lookup contents, history or visitor information will never be sold to anyone. That constraint is spelled out in the privacy policy.
